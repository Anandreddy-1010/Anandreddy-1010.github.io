About

Grounded in math, shipped in code.

I'm Kummetha Anand Reddy, a third-year B.Tech Computer Science (AI & ML) student at Dayananda Sagar University, currently holding a 9.47/10.0 CGPA. My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and real engineering — I care less about a model that scores well in a notebook and more about one that survives contact with real data, real hardware, and real users.

That mindset shaped CropSense AI, a MobileNetV2-based crop disease classifier trained on 54,000+ images that I pushed all the way to a live Streamlit app with a four-layer fallback system and sub-50ms inference on a Raspberry Pi. It also shaped my renewable energy optimizer, where I combined time-series forecasting with interactive 3D visualisation to make solar and wind data genuinely readable.

Day to day, I work across Python, NumPy, Pandas, and scikit-learn/TensorFlow, with a strong grip on the statistics and linear algebra underneath the models — the same fundamentals the Amazon ML Summer School selection test is built around. I round it out with front-end skills (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js) so I can ship what I build, not just prototype it.
